About DPG School of Technology & Management

DPG School of Technology & Management (DPG STM) is a premier higher education institution located in Gurugram, Haryana. The institute offers comprehensive undergraduate and postgraduate programs in Computer Applications, Management, Commerce, and related disciplines.

Affiliated with Maharshi Dayanand University (MDU), Rohtak, DPG STM is dedicated to providing quality education that combines academic rigor with practical industry skills. The institution serves as a bridge between traditional education and modern professional requirements.

This website is an unofficial, student-created informational page developed for learning, academic support, and community purposes. It does not represent the official administration of the institution. For official information, please visit the official DPG STM website.

Academic Programs & Departments

DPG STM (School of Technology and Management) offers diverse academic programs designed to develop skilled professionals across multiple domains:

  • Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA): A three-year undergraduate program focusing on software development, web technologies, database management, and emerging technologies like AI & ML.
  • Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA): Comprehensive business education covering management principles, finance, marketing, and organizational behavior.
  • Bachelor of Commerce (B.Com): Specialized commerce program with emphasis on accounting, finance, taxation, and business law.
  • Master of Computer Applications (MCA): Advanced postgraduate program in software engineering and advanced computing.
  • Master of Business Administration (MBA): Executive-level management education with specializations in Finance, Marketing, and Operations.
  • B.Tech Specializations: Including AI & ML, Data Science, Cloud Computing, and Cybersecurity.
  • Vocational & Certification Courses: Short-term programs in Financial Planning, Business Analytics, Full Stack Development, and Digital Marketing.

The campus features modern classrooms, laboratory facilities, libraries, and digital learning infrastructure to support both theoretical knowledge and practical skill development. Internal assessments, practical labs, seminars, and university examinations are conducted under MDU guidelines.

Campus Life & Learning Culture

Student life at DPG STM reflects a vibrant balance between academics, skill-building, extracurricular activities, and personal growth. The institution fosters an inclusive learning environment where curiosity and innovation are encouraged.

Academic Routine: Students engage in regular lectures, practical sessions, internal tests, project work, and collaborative learning. Subject experts and industry professionals conduct seminars and workshops to bridge the gap between classroom learning and real-world applications.

Holistic Development: Beyond coursework, students participate in cultural events, technical competitions, sports activities, and clubs that develop leadership, teamwork, and communication skills.

Digital Learning Infrastructure: An online ERP portal enables students to track attendance, access course materials uploaded by faculty, submit assignments, and monitor academic progress in real-time.

This personal blog space serves as an extension of the DPG STM community, sharing academic tools, generators, learning utilities, and student insights to support academic success.

Admissions & Enrollment

DPG STM welcomes qualified candidates across India and abroad to join its growing academic community. The admission process is designed to identify talented and motivated individuals passionate about learning and professional development.

Admission Requirements vary by program:

  • UG Programs (BCA, BBA, B.Com): 10+2 or equivalent qualification; merit-based or entrance exam scores.
  • PG Programs (MCA, MBA): Bachelor's degree in relevant field; some programs require entrance examinations or GMAT/CAT scores.
  • Merit & Scholarships: DPG STM offers scholarships based on academic excellence and financial need to deserving students.

For detailed admission procedures, eligibility criteria, application deadlines, and fee structures, candidates are encouraged to visit the official DPG STM admissions portal or contact the admissions office directly.
